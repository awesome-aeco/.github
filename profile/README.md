### What We Maintain

- **[Awesome-AECO](https://github.com/awesome-aeco/Awesome-AECO)** — 200+ curated open-source tools across BIM & IFC, CAD, Revit extensions, simulation & FEA, energy & sustainability, parametric design, digital twins, GIS, AI/MCP agents, and cost estimation.
- **Machine-Readable & AI-Ready** — Structured Markdown, build-time `ItemList` / `FAQPage` JSON-LD, [`llms.txt`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/awesome-aeco/Awesome-AECO/main/llms.txt), and [`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/awesome-aeco/Awesome-AECO/blob/main/AGENTS.md) for AI-assisted contributions.

### Contributing

Know an open-source or publicly usable AECO tool that belongs in the directory? Read our [contribution guidelines](https://github.com/awesome-aeco/Awesome-AECO/blob/main/contributing.md) and open a pull request. Listed projects can display the [`Featured in Awesome AECO`](https://github.com/awesome-aeco/Awesome-AECO/blob/main/contributing.md#featured-badge) badge.
